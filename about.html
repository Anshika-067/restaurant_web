Our story

A table for every
kind of curious.

The Food Hunt started with a notebook full of places we wanted to eat and recipes we wanted to try. Today, it is our warm little corner of Indiranagar: a place for produce-led plates, good coffee, and conversations that run past dessert.

Our menu moves with the markets and our mood. Nothing is too precious, but everything gets our full attention.
